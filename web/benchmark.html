test-09

Which attributes describe a business customer in the common Account definition?

Reference answer

The common Account definition includes accountCategoryCode, which distinguishes standard from preferred accounts, and customerSizeCode, which records an account size category for segmentation [applicationCommon::Account].

Expected entities
  • applicationCommon::Account

test-10

Where can I find first and last name fields for an individual contact?

Reference answer

The common Contact definition has firstName and lastName attributes for the contact's first and last names [applicationCommon::Contact].

Expected entities
  • applicationCommon::Contact

Custom (not in test) · 2 questions

complex-funds-01

Compare how an overdraft, standing order, and direct debit move money: which provides credit after a checking account reaches zero, which transfers a fixed amount at regular intervals, and which collects payments with the customer's authorization?

Reference answer

FIOverdraft is credit linked to a checking account that allows withdrawals after its balance reaches zero [RetailBankingCoreDataModel::FIOverdraft]. FIStandingOrder instructs that a fixed amount be removed from one account at regular intervals and paid or transferred to another [RetailBankingCoreDataModel::FIStandingOrder]. FIDirectDebit lets a customer authorize a creditor to debit the customer's bank account at regular intervals [RetailBankingCoreDataModel::FIDirectDebit].

Expected entities
  • RetailBankingCoreDataModel::FIOverdraft
  • RetailBankingCoreDataModel::FIStandingOrder
  • RetailBankingCoreDataModel::FIDirectDebit

complex-customer-links-01

How do the common Account, Contact, and CustomerRelationship entities describe a customer relationship? Identify what Account and Contact represent, explain what CustomerRelationship represents, and name the field that connects it to an Account or Contact.

Reference answer

Account represents a customer or potential customer and the company billed in business transactions [applicationCommon::Account]. Contact represents a person with whom a business unit has a relationship [applicationCommon::Contact]. CustomerRelationship represents a relationship between a customer and a partner, either of which can be an Account or Contact; its partner field references the Account or Contact [applicationCommon::CustomerRelationship].

Expected entities
  • applicationCommon::Account
  • applicationCommon::Contact
  • applicationCommon::CustomerRelationship

Test · 2 questions

paired-test-customer-partner

What do a common Account and Contact represent, and which CustomerRelationship field can reference either of them?

Reference answer

An applicationCommon Account represents a customer or potential customer and the billed company. A Contact is a person with whom a business unit has a relationship. CustomerRelationship.partner can reference either Account or Contact.

Expected entities
  • applicationCommon::Account
  • applicationCommon::Contact
  • applicationCommon::CustomerRelationship

paired-test-card-holding-roles

What schema identifies the holder of a card, and where are the roles a customer may have in a financial holding defined?

Reference answer

FICard.Cardholder identifies the customer associated with the card and references Account or Contact. CustomerFinancialHolding.financialHoldingRole records the customer's role, such as owner or beneficiary. Its data-type trait records values including Owner, Cardholder, Signatory, Co-signer, Joint owner, Beneficiary and Guarantor.

Expected entities
  • RetailBankingCoreDataModel::FICard
  • RetailBankingCoreDataModel::CustomerFinancialHolding
Development (used for tuning) · 4 questions

paired-dev-group-holdings

How does the model associate financial holdings with a household group and with individual customers, and what can those holdings include?

Reference answer

GroupFinancialHolding has Group and FinancialHolding links. CustomerFinancialHolding links a customer to a FinancialHolding. Financial holdings include accounts, loans, investments, credit lines and savings accounts.

Expected entities
  • RetailBankingCoreDataModel::GroupFinancialHolding
  • RetailBankingCoreDataModel::CustomerFinancialHolding
  • RetailBankingCoreDataModel::FinancialHolding

paired-dev-card-customer

In the banking model, what can a cardholder refer to, and how does that relate to the customer linked to a financial holding?

Reference answer

FICard.Cardholder can reference an Account or Contact. CustomerFinancialHolding.Customer can also reference an Account or Contact, and CustomerFinancialHolding.FinancialHolding references a FinancialHolding. These links do not by themselves establish that the two customers are the same record.

Expected entities
  • RetailBankingCoreDataModel::FICard
  • RetailBankingCoreDataModel::CustomerFinancialHolding

paired-dev-holding-details

How are customer holdings connected to account, loan and investment details, and what does each detail type represent?

Reference answer

FinancialHolding.Details references FHAccount, FHLoan and FHInvestment among other types. FHAccount is a deposit account supporting deposits and withdrawals; FHLoan describes loans held by a customer with a financial institution; FHInvestment represents a portfolio of securities or other investible assets.

Expected entities
  • RetailBankingCoreDataModel::FinancialHolding
  • RetailBankingCoreDataModel::FHAccount
  • RetailBankingCoreDataModel::FHLoan
  • RetailBankingCoreDataModel::FHInvestment

paired-dev-common-account-contact

How can a common Account refer to its primary Contact, and how can a Contact identify a parent customer?

Reference answer

The applicationCommon Account.primaryContact relationship references Contact. Contact.parentCustomer can reference either Account or Contact.

Expected entities
  • applicationCommon::Account
  • applicationCommon::Contact
