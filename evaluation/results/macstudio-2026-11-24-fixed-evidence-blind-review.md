# Fixed-evidence answer review: blind assessment

The shuffled review worksheet was assessed before opening its model/arm key. The worksheet includes each question, draft, original complete passages and provisional reference checklist. Evidence support means support by the cited passage, not proof the claim is globally false. Missing evidence and missing answer content are recorded separately. Coverage is assessed against the actual question; examples in the reference are a checklist rather than exhaustive ground truth. One reviewer assessed 24 generated drafts; repeated drafts and two arms from the same question are not independent questions. No model judge was used.

## Scope and limitations

The fixed-evidence exercise isolates answer generation given previously retrieved passages. It cannot show that a model improves retrieval, the reformulation, or end-to-end performance. In several cases the retrieved passages cannot support a complete answer; an explicit limitation is preferable to filling gaps. A source's text can be relevant even when its citation metadata is inaccurate, and ambiguous or merely imprecise wording is identified separately from a definite wrong edge.
